# Claims & Evidence appendix

Every claim in the write-up and dashboard, the table and query behind it, the result, the alternative explanation we tested, and the limitation. Table names refer to the gold layer in `src/pipeline/*.sql`; all of them are rebuilt by `python local/run_local.py` or by the Databricks job.

| # | Claim | Table / query | Result | Alternative tested | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Two of the manager's 10 are already retired; one is a new install | `gold_machine_scorecard` where `manager_rank <= 10` (`retire_date`, `days_on_floor`) | M-1118 retired Feb 25, M-1120 May 13; M-5076 installed May 13 (49 days) | They might still be weak per day: M-1118 fair index 1.10 (fine), M-1120/M-5076 ≈ 0.5 (low-hold Royal Draw) | Ranking by raw total penalises partial periods by construction |
| 2 | M-1158 pays off spec | `gold_machine_scorecard`: `hit_rate_z`, `luck_z`; weekly hit rate from `silver_machine_day` | Hit rate 38.6% vs 31.5% spec over 674,454 spins (z = +124); hold 3.0% vs 10% theo (luck z = −4.6) | Bad luck: the hit-rate excess is stable every week Jan–Jun, which luck cannot do; no other machine exceeds \|z\| 3.6 (Bonferroni cut-off for 310 tests) | A lead to inspect, not proof of tampering; could be a misconfigured paytable |
| 3 | Back Forty's low earnings are location | `gold_machine_scorecard.location_usd`; `gold_natural_experiments` | Location component −$26.7K per Back Forty machine; walkway closure Mar 3–Apr 6: −29.2% coin-in and −28.9% door entries vs other zones | Seasonality/citywide demand: controlled by the rest-of-floor comparison over the same days | One closure; signage lift is extrapolated, not observed |
| 4 | The truly weak earners are Royal Draw machines | `gold_machine_scorecard.fair_index`, `demand_index`, `hold_setting_usd` | 25 Royal Draws average fair index 0.56; demand index ≈ 1.06 | Players might prefer a 95% paytable: demand index ≈ 1.0 shows no extra play | Theo-based index assumes a replacement title attracts par play |
| 5 | New titles can reach ≥ par without cannibalising | `gold_machine_scorecard` (Aurora Link), `gold_natural_experiments` (Aurora row) | 8 Aurora Link machines at 1.37× par, stable Mar–Jun; neighbours +1.6% vs other zones | Novelty effect: monthly index 1.40 → 1.35 → 1.37 → 1.38, no decay | One title, one zone |
| 6 | Downtime cost $102K theo (0.4%) | `gold_downtime_lost` | 5,021 machine-hours; $102,269 theo; Ridgeline $56.6K | Valuing at daily average would overstate night outages: valued by zone × local hour × weekday/weekend | Assumes a broken machine's players would have played at the zone's rate |
| 7 | Extra grave tech is not worth it | `gold_wait_vs_staffing`, `gold_repair_response`, `gold_action_options` | Grave wait 1.96 h vs 0.09 h with two techs; ≈ $16 theo per grave downtime hour → ≈ $944 July benefit vs $11,520 | Short staffing drives waits (0-tech day shifts wait 4.9 h): true, but the hours are cheap | Ignores guest-experience cost of broken machines |
| 8 | Tracked results are concentrated | `gold_player_concentration` | Top 1% = 20% of theo, top 5% = 52%; 16% of players finished ahead | Concentration driven by luck: bands are ranked by theo (luck-free), not by net | Carded play only (≈ 66% of coin-in) |
| 9 | Comps got 55% cheaper on May 1 | `gold_comp_price_day`, `gold_comp_month` | 12.3 → 5.7 points per comp dollar overnight (Apr 30 → May 1); same step in every tier | Gradual drift: Jan–Apr drift is ≈ 3%/month; the May 1 step is 53% in one day | Reason for the change is not in the data |
| 10 | No detectable play lift from the comp change | `gold_comp_change_test` | Carded vs uncarded coin-in +2.4%; carded share +0.5 pt vs month-to-month SD ±0.7 pt | It might be a real small lift: cannot be ruled out, hence the comp pilot as next step | Two post-change months |
